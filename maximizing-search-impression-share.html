Maximizing Search Impression Share

How to optimize your bidding strategy to win competitive keyword auctions on Google Ads without wasting budget.

Search Impression Share (SIS) is one of the most critical health metrics for any Google Ads search campaign. It represents the percentage of impressions your ads received compared to the total number of impressions your ads were eligible to receive.

If your impression share is low, you are leaving money on the table. In this article, we'll examine how to identify the bottlenecks restricting your ad delivery and how to resolve them.

1. Identify the Cause: Budget vs. Rank

Google breaks down lost impression share into two distinct metrics: **Search Lost IS (budget)** and **Search Lost IS (rank)**. Before modifying your campaigns, you must check your columns to see where the loss is occurring.

2. Optimizing Lost IS (Rank)

If you are losing share due to rank, your ad position or quality score is too low to compete in the auction. To fix this:

  • **Improve Ad Relevance:** Make sure your search keywords appear dynamically in your headlines and ad copy.
  • **Landing Page Experience:** Ensure the landing page loads fast and provides the exact answer the searcher is looking for.
  • **Adjust Bids:** If using manual bidding, raise your CPC bids, or switch to a target impression share bidding strategy.

3. Optimizing Lost IS (Budget)

If you are losing share due to budget, your campaigns are running out of daily budget before the day ends. To fix this, you don't necessarily have to spend more money. Instead, try narrowing your target parameters (e.g., location, ad scheduling, exact-match keywords) to ensure your limited budget is spent only on the highest-converting searches.
